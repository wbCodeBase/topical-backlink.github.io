How it works

From brief to live link in five steps

Hover to pause, or pick any step to jump to it.

01 WITHIN 72 HOURS

Tell us the domain. We map the gap.

Send the client domain, target pages and any anchors or topics to avoid. We map their link graph against their three closest competitors and return the exact domains they're missing.

  • Competitor link-gap report, white-labelled
  • Recommended target pages and anchor mix
02 YOU SIGN OFF

See every site before we pitch it.

The domain, its authority, traffic and price land in your approval queue first. Nothing is pitched without a yes from you, so nothing surprises your client.

  • Vetted prospect list with DR, traffic and price
  • One-click approve or reject per domain
03 WEEKS 1–3

Real pitches, written for each publication.

Our outreach team pitches editors one by one, and our writers produce content that fits each site's audience. Your client's link sits where an editor would naturally place it.

  • Individually pitched, never templated blasts
  • Drafts available for review on request
04 FROM WEEK 3

Links land, and your dashboard shows them.

When a placement publishes, it appears on the branded dashboard immediately, with the live URL, DR, estimated traffic, anchor text and target page. A typical order completes inside 30 days.

  • Real-time status: queued, outreach, content, live
  • Client login under your own domain
05 ONGOING

Monitored for a year, replaced if lost.

Every live link is monitored continuously. If one drops, is nofollowed or the page is pulled, we replace it on a comparable domain at no charge, and your client never has to ask.

  • 12-month replacement guarantee
  • Monthly branded PDF if your client prefers one
