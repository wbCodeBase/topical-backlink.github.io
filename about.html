OUR STORY

Built out of frustration with the alternative

We started in 2019 running SEO for agencies who kept getting burned by link vendors: placements on sites with no traffic, links that disappeared after ninety days, and reports that could not be checked.

So we built the opposite. Every prospect is scored before it is pitched. Every placement is a real editorial decision by a real editor. Every link is visible in a dashboard the moment it goes live, and guaranteed for a year.

Today around 3,500 agencies run us as their link-building department across 52 countries and 183 industries.

How we operate

Three rules we do not bend

If it would take anything, it is worth nothing

A site that publishes whatever it is paid to publish passes no authority. We would rather come back with four placements than forty from a network.

Show the work, always

You see the domain, the authority and the price before we pitch, and the live URL the moment it publishes. No monthly PDF standing between you and the truth.

Say no to the wrong brief

If your problem is technical, or your content is not ready, links will not fix it. We will tell you that on the first call rather than take the retainer.
